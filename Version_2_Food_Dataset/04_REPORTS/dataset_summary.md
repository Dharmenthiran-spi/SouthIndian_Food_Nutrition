# 📊 Version 2 Master Food & Nutrition Dataset Summary

The **Version 2 Master Food & Nutrition Dataset** encompasses **229 verified food entries** across **17 categories**, accompanied by **78 component-level recipes** and a standalone local **SQLite database**.

### Directory Structure Overview
```
Version_2_Food_Dataset/
├── 01_RAW_DATA/           # 18 category raw archives & RAW_DATA_MANIFEST.csv
├── 02_PROCESSED_DATA/     # Cleaned, deduplicated, and normalized CSV/JSON files
├── 03_FINAL_DATA/         # Application-ready foods_master.csv/.json & food_database.sqlite
└── 04_REPORTS/            # Validation, category summaries, and license reports
```
